# Guide

Two how to documents for the two jobs that come up most often on this ship.

- [adding-a-relay.md](adding-a-relay.md) is how to put another computer on the
  radio so it reports something the flight computer cannot see for itself.
- [gui.md](gui.md) is how to draw a screen that looks like the rest of them.

`README.md` at the root of the repo says what the ship is. `CLAUDE.md` is the
house rules and the map of the folders. These two are the walkthroughs, so they
repeat a little of both on purpose: a walkthrough that sends you to another
file for the step in the middle is a walkthrough nobody finishes.
